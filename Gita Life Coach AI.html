Chapter 2, Verse 47

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

Translation

"You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action. Never consider yourself the cause of the results of your activities, nor be attached to inaction."

Modern Psychological Breakdown

Anxiety stems from outcome obsession. By focusing entirely on the quality of your present effort (process) rather than external validation or rewards (outcome), you achieve peak performance and mental tranquility.
